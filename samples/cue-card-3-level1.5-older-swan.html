Level 1.5, older clients, swan
Level 1.5, older clients, 50 min. Peak: Swan (Swan on the long box)
1. Footwork7 min, Reformer

Springs: Heavy, 3 red
Heels wide, toes out (ER); Knees open over toes; Inner thighs zip on return
Footwork: calf stretch under the bar1 min, 3 slow holds
Springs: Heavy, 3 red
Legs long, heels under FB; Hold 3 breaths, lift, repeat; Soft knees if tight
Footwork: toes, parallel2 min, x10
Springs: Heavy, 3 red
Balls of feet on FB, par; Heels lifted, still ankles; Press out, return slow
Footwork: toes, Pilates V2 min, x10
Springs: Heavy, 3 red
Heels together, toes apart (V); Turn out from the hips; Squeeze inner thighs out

Hinge bridge, feet on footbar2 min, x8

Arches on FB, hip wd; Lift hips as one piece; Hold, lower in one piece
Bridge with magic circle2 min, x8
Springs: Heavy, 3 red
MC between knees; Lift hips, squeeze gently; Lower slow, keep squeeze
3. Abs4 min, Reformer
Chest lift, feet on footbar1½ min, x8
Springs: Heavy, 3 red
Arches on FB, hands behind head; Exh, HNS curl up; Inh hold, Exh lower

Springs: Light, 1 red
Feet in straps, heels together; HNS, hands behind head; Press legs out, bend in

Springs: Light, 1 red

Sit on LB, back to straps; Arms wide, soft elbows; Hug forward, open slow

Key. HNS Head and shoulders lifted; FB Footbar; par Parallel legs; V Pilates V, heels together, toes apart; ER External rotation, turned out; ea Each side; x Repetitions, so x8 means 8 times; Inh Inhale; Exh Exhale; sit bns Sit bones; LB Long box; SB Short box (box turned the short way); hip wd Hip width apart; sec Seconds; wts Light weights; MC Magic circle. Springs: red heavy, blue medium, yellow light. Older clients start lighter. Start at the lighter load. Add spring only if form stays clean.
