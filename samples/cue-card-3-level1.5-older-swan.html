Key. TT Tabletop, legs at 90 degrees; FB Footbar; par Parallel legs; ea Each side; x Repetitions, so x8 means 8 times; sit bns Sit bones; LB Long box; SB Short box (box turned the short way); art Articulate, one bone at a time; sec Seconds; wts Light weights; MC Magic circle. Springs: red heavy, blue medium, yellow light. Older clients start lighter. Start at the lighter load. Add spring only if form stays clean.
