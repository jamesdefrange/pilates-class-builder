Key. HNS Head and shoulders lifted; FB Footbar; carr Carriage; par Parallel legs; ER External rotation, turned out; ea Each side; x Repetitions, so x8 means 8 times; Inh Inhale; Exh Exhale; scaps Shoulder blades; hip wd Hip width apart; art Articulate, one bone at a time; sec Seconds; MC Magic circle.
